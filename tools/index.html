Web tools

DropDial’s most-needed tools, in your browser. On an iPhone, Android phone, iPad, Mac or PC, with nothing to install.

Your files never leave this device. DropDial’s web tools run in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

Images

  • Convert imageHEIC, PNG, WebP, JPEG and more
  • Resize imagePixels, percent or file size
  • Make image smallerSmaller JPEG, PNG and WebP files
  • Crop & rotateShapes, quarter turns and flips
  • Images to PDFOne PDF from pictures, in order
  • Remove location & private detailsLocation, camera and dates, gone
  • Read QR codeLinks, text and UPI payments

PDF

  • Combine PDFsJoin PDFs in the order you choose
  • Split PDFPages, ranges or every N
  • Arrange PDF pagesReorder, rotate and delete
  • Shrink PDFLight or strong compression
  • Add page numbersSix positions, three styles
  • Watermark PDFText, diagonal or tiled
  • PDF to imagesEvery page as a JPEG or PNG
  • Remove PDF passwordAn unlocked copy, with your password

Video

  • Convert videoMOV, MKV and WebM to MP4
  • Make video smallerFor WhatsApp, email and uploads
  • Trim videoKeep just the part you want
  • Video to GIFGIF or animated WebP from a clip
  • Remove soundA silent copy of a video
  • Extract audioThe sound of a video as M4A or MP3
  • Change video speedFrom 0.25× to 4×

Audio

  • Convert audioMP3, M4A, WAV, FLAC and OGG
  • Trim audioCut the start and end, with fades
  • Make audio smallerSmaller MP3, M4A and WAV files

Documents and sheets

  • PDF to text or WordThe words of a PDF, ready to edit
  • Word to textPlain text from a DOCX file
  • Text to WordTXT or Markdown as a DOCX
  • Convert spreadsheetCSV, Excel and JSON, any way round

No web tool matches. The DropDial app has many more tools.

How the web tools work

  1. Choose your filesOr drop them on the page. On a phone, pick them from Photos or Files.
  2. Pick the optionsEach tool starts with sensible choices, and shows a preview where it helps.
  3. Download the resultsOne at a time, all together as a ZIP, or share them straight from your phone.

The work happens on your device with the same code as the DropDial app; video and audio use your browser’s own encoders. Big files take longer on a phone, and the video and audio tools need a recent browser (Safari 16.4 or later; Chrome, Edge or Firefox from 2024 on). For every format and the full toolbox, get the app for Mac or Windows.
