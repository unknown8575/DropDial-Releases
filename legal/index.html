Privacy

Your files stay on your computer unless you send them with Nearby. DropDial has no account, no cloud, no ads and no tracking. It does not collect, send or sell any data about you or your files.

  • Every conversion, tool and analysis runs on this computer. Nothing is uploaded to the internet.
  • Bank statements are read in a locked-down sandbox. Passwords you type to open a PDF are used only to open it and are never saved.
  • DropDial saves only your settings (like where to save files) on this computer. While it is running, Activity lists this session’s jobs, with file names, in memory only. That list is never written to disk and is gone when you quit DropDial or press Clear finished.
  • DropDial has no analytics and no crash reporting. The only time it goes online by itself is the update check: once a day it reads a small file from DropDial’s releases on GitHub (turn this off in Settings → General). Nothing about you or your files is sent, and an update is downloaded only when you click Update.
  • Nearby sharing is off until you turn it on. When it is on, DropDial tells other computers on your local network its name. Files go to another computer only when you send them, straight across that network and encrypted end to end. Nothing is received until you accept it.

Terms of use

  • DropDial is free to use, for personal and work use.
  • Converting and the tools never change your originals; results are saved as new files. Only the file actions Rename… and Move to… change your files, and Rename can be undone in Activity. Still, keep backups of files that matter.
  • Bank statement analysis is for your information only. Payee names and categories are guessed from transaction descriptions and can be wrong. It is not financial, tax or legal advice: check important figures against your bank statement.
  • Only process files you have the right to use.
  • DropDial is provided “as is”, without any warranty. To the extent the law allows, the author is not liable for any loss or damage from using it.

This website

This website has no cookies, analytics or trackers and loads nothing from other sites. The Download page asks GitHub’s public API for the latest version; GitHub sees that request like any page visit.

The web tools run in your browser. The files you choose are read and changed on your device and are never uploaded, to DropDial or anyone else. Nothing about them is stored, and closing the page clears them.

Besides the app’s own components, the video and audio web tools use Mediabunny (MPL-2.0), the LAME MP3 encoder (LGPL), FFmpeg’s AAC encoder (LGPL, only in browsers without one of their own) and libFLAC (BSD), each loaded only when a tool needs it. Their licenses are in Licenses and notices.
