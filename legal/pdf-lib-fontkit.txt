@pdf-lib/fontkit 1.1.1 (a fork of fontkit by Devon Govett, maintained for pdf-lib)
https://github.com/Hopding/fontkit

DropDial ships dist/fontkit.umd.min.js with two small changes made by scripts/vendor.js: the Indic shaper's state
machine is written as a native generator (the build expected Babel's regeneratorRuntime), and the TrueType subsetter
pads each copied glyph to an even length (it can write a short loca table).
