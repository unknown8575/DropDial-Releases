Tesseract language data: English (eng) and Hindi (hin), "4.0.0_best_int"
From the npm packages @tesseract.js-data/eng 1.0.0 and @tesseract.js-data/hin 1.0.0 (https://github.com/naptha/tessdata),
shipped unchanged as src/processor/vendor/tesseract/lang/eng.traineddata.gz and hin.traineddata.gz.

The data are the LSTM models of the Tesseract project's tessdata_best (https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_best),
converted to integer by the Tesseract.js project. The Tesseract project's language data are licensed under the Apache
