## The website's video and audio tools

The DropDial website's video and audio tools (/tools/) also use the components below, in the visitor's browser.
Each is loaded only when a tool needs it, and their license texts are in this folder (/legal/).

| Component | Version | License | Use | License text |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mediabunny | 1.66.0 | MPL-2.0 | Reading and writing MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV, MP3, M4A, WAV and OGG; drives the browser's own video and audio encoders (WebCodecs) | mediabunny.txt |
| @mediabunny/mp3-encoder (LAME 3.100) | 1.66.0 | MPL-2.0 (LAME: LGPL-2.0 or later) | Making MP3 files | mediabunny.txt, lame.txt |
| @mediabunny/aac-encoder (FFmpeg's AAC encoder, LGPL build) | 1.66.0 | MPL-2.0 (FFmpeg: LGPL-2.1 or later) | Making AAC sound where the browser has no AAC encoder | mediabunny.txt, ffmpeg-aac.txt |
| @mediabunny/flac-encoder (libFLAC) | 1.66.0 | MPL-2.0 (libFLAC: BSD-3-Clause) | Making FLAC files | mediabunny.txt, libflac.txt |
| gifenc | 1.0.3 | MIT | GIFs from videos (the app's copy) | gifenc.txt |

LAME, FFmpeg's AAC encoder and libFLAC are WebAssembly builds made by Mediabunny's authors from unmodified sources
(see lame.txt, ffmpeg-aac.txt and libflac.txt for the sources and build steps). Each runs from its own file
(/tools/engine/media/mp3-worker.js, aac-worker.js, flac-worker.js) and can be replaced. No GPL code is used by the
website.
